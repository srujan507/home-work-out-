📅 Weekly Schedule — One Muscle Focus Per Day

Each day targets specific muscle groups. Sunday is full recovery — rest is where growth happens. Always warm up 5 minutes before and cool down/stretch 5 minutes after every session. Stay hydrated throughout the day. After Week 4, this same weekly cycle repeats indefinitely.

📚 Exercise Library — By Muscle Group

Every exercise lists the muscles affected, step-by-step instructions, sets/reps/rest, difficulty, and a household alternative if you need one. Expand a muscle group below.

💥 Chest

Wall Push-up Beginner

Muscles affected: Pectorals (chest), front deltoids, triceps
  1. Stand arm's length from a wall, palms flat on it at shoulder height, slightly wider than shoulders.
  2. Lean in, keeping body straight from head to heels.
  3. Bend elbows to lower chest toward the wall.
  4. Push back to start. Keep core tight.
3 sets × 12–15 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: none needed — it IS the household version.

Knee Push-up Beginner

Muscles affected: Pectorals, front deltoids, triceps, core
  1. Start on hands and knees, hands slightly wider than shoulders.
  2. Lower chest toward the floor with elbows at ~45°.
  3. Push back up until arms are straight.
3 sets × 8–12 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: push-up against a sturdy table edge.

Standard Push-up Intermediate

Muscles affected: Pectorals, front deltoids, triceps, serratus anterior, core
  1. High plank position, hands under shoulders, body in a straight line.
  2. Lower chest to just above the floor, elbows at ~45°.
  3. Drive through palms to press back up. No sagging hips.
3–4 sets × 10–15 reps · Rest 60–90s
🏠 Alternative: knee push-ups or incline push-ups on a chair.

Diamond Push-up Advanced

Muscles affected: Inner pectorals, triceps, front deltoids
  1. Form a diamond/triangle with thumbs and index fingers under your chest.
  2. Lower chest to hands, keeping elbows tucked in.
  3. Press back up explosively.
4 sets × 8–12 reps · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: narrow-grip knee push-ups.

Chair/Bench Triceps Dip Intermediate

Muscles affected: Triceps, lower pectorals, front deltoids
  1. Hands on a chair edge behind you, legs extended forward, heels down.
  2. Lower hips by bending elbows to ~90°.
  3. Press back up through palms. Keep shoulders down.
3 sets × 10–15 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: bend knees to reduce load.
🏋️ Back

Superman Hold Beginner

Muscles affected: Lower back (erector spinae), glutes, rear deltoids
  1. Lie face down, arms extended forward, legs straight.
  2. Squeeze glutes and lift chest, arms, and legs off the floor.
  3. Hold 2–3 seconds, lower with control.
3 sets × 12 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: bird-dog on hands and knees.

Towel Row (Door Anchor) Beginner

Muscles affected: Latissimus dorsi, rhomboids, mid-trapezius, rear deltoids, biceps
  1. Loop a towel around a sturdy door handle or pole.
  2. Hold both ends, lean back with straight arms, feet planted.
  3. Pull your chest toward the door by bending elbows back.
  4. Lower slowly.
3 sets × 12 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: backpack loaded with books, bent-over rows.

Pull-up / Negative Pull-up Intermediate

Muscles affected: Lats, mid-traps, rhomboids, biceps, forearms, core
  1. Hang from a bar, grip slightly wider than shoulders.
  2. Pull chest toward the bar, squeezing shoulder blades.
  3. Lower with control. (Beginners: jump up and lower slowly = negatives.)
3 sets × 5–10 reps · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: door-frame rows or towel rows.

Archer Pull-up Advanced

Muscles affected: Lats (unilateral), biceps, rear deltoids, core
  1. Wide grip hang. Pull toward one hand while the opposite arm straightens.
  2. Squeeze, return to center.
  3. Alternate sides each rep.
4 sets × 4–6 reps/side · Rest 120s
🏠 Alternative: weighted one-arm backpack rows.
🎯 Shoulders

Pike Push-up Beginner

Muscles affected: Front & side deltoids, triceps, upper trapezius
  1. From push-up position, walk feet in and lift hips into an inverted V.
  2. Lower the top of your head toward the floor between your hands.
  3. Press back up. Keep hips high.
3 sets × 8–12 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: wall handstand hold (short holds).

Shoulder Taps Beginner

Muscles affected: Deltoids, core stabilizers, chest
  1. Hold a high plank, feet wide for stability.
  2. Tap your left shoulder with your right hand, then swap.
  3. Keep hips as still as possible.
3 sets × 16 taps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: do it on knees.

Elevated Pike Push-up Intermediate

Muscles affected: All three deltoid heads, triceps, upper traps
  1. Feet elevated on a chair in pike position.
  2. Lower head between hands until it nearly touches the floor.
  3. Press back up, fully extending arms.
4 sets × 8–10 reps · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: standard pike push-up.

Wall Handstand Push-up Advanced

Muscles affected: Deltoids, triceps, trapezius, core, upper back
  1. Kick up into a handstand against a wall, arms locked.
  2. Bend elbows to lower your head toward the floor.
  3. Press back up to full lockout.
4 sets × 4–8 reps · Rest 120s
🏠 Alternative: elevated pike push-ups.
💪 Biceps

Towel Curl (Isometric) Beginner

Muscles affected: Biceps brachii, forearm flexors
  1. Loop a towel under one foot, hold both ends, elbow braced on thigh.
  2. Curl your hand up while pulling down with the leg for resistance.
  3. Slow 3-second lowering phase.
3 sets × 12 reps/arm · Rest 45s
🏠 Alternative: filled water bottles or backpack curls.

Backpack Curl Intermediate

Muscles affected: Biceps, forearm flexors
  1. Grip a loaded backpack by its handles.
  2. Curl from full extension to shoulder height.
  3. Squeeze at the top 1 second, lower for 3 seconds.
3–4 sets × 10–12 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: two large water jugs.

One-Armed Backpack Curl Advanced

Muscles affected: Biceps (unilateral), forearm flexors, grip
  1. Single-arm heavy backpack curl with a 3-second negative.
  2. Keep elbow pinned to your side.
  3. Full range, no swinging.
4 sets × 6–8 reps/arm · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: slow-tempo two-arm curl with both jugs.
🔧 Triceps

Bench/Chair Dip Beginner

Muscles affected: Triceps, front deltoids, lower chest
  1. Hands on chair edge, knees bent, feet flat.
  2. Bend elbows to ~90°, keeping them pointing back.
  3. Press up through palms.
3 sets × 10–12 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: wall push-offs.

Close-Grip Push-up Intermediate

Muscles affected: Triceps, inner chest, front deltoids
  1. Hands directly under shoulders (narrower than normal).
  2. Lower with elbows brushing your ribs.
  3. Press up, locking out fully.
3 sets × 10–15 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: knee close-grip push-ups.

Dive-Bomber Push-up Advanced

Muscles affected: Triceps, shoulders, chest, core
  1. Start in downward-dog position.
  2. Swoop your chest forward close to the floor until arms are vertical.
  3. Press back and return along the same arc.
4 sets × 8–10 reps · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: decline close-grip push-up (feet on chair).
✊ Forearms

Towel Wring Squeeze Beginner

Muscles affected: Forearm flexors, grip muscles
  1. Hold a wet/damp towel with both hands.
  2. Wring it hard in opposite directions for 20–30 seconds.
  3. Rest and repeat, switching twist direction.
3 sets × 30s · Rest 45s
🏠 Alternative: squeeze a tennis ball.

Dead Hang Intermediate

Muscles affected: Forearm flexors, grip, lats, shoulder stabilizers
  1. Hang from a pull-up bar with straight arms.
  2. Squeeze the bar hard, keep shoulders active.
  3. Hold as long as possible with good form.
3 sets × 30–45s · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: door-frame towel hangs.

One-Arm Hang Advanced

Muscles affected: Grip, forearms, lats, core
  1. Hang from the bar with one arm only.
  2. Engage lats and core to stop swinging.
  3. Hold for time, then switch arms.
3 sets × 15–25s/arm · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: two-arm hang with a backpack on.
🦵 Quadriceps

Bodyweight Squat Beginner

Muscles affected: Quadriceps, glutes, hamstrings, calves, core
  1. Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out.
  2. Sit back and down like reaching for a chair, chest up.
  3. Descend until thighs are at least parallel, then drive through heels.
3 sets × 12–15 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: sit-to-stand from a chair.

Walking Lunges Intermediate

Muscles affected: Quads, glutes, hamstrings, calves, core
  1. Step forward into a long stride.
  2. Lower until both knees are ~90°, front knee over ankle.
  3. Push through the front heel and bring the back leg forward.
3 sets × 10/leg · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: stationary alternating lunges.

Pistol Squat Advanced

Muscles affected: Quads, glutes, hamstrings, ankle stabilizers, core
  1. Stand on one leg, other leg extended straight ahead.
  2. Squat all the way down on one leg, keeping chest up and heel down.
  3. Drive back up without the other foot touching.
4 sets × 3–6 reps/leg · Rest 120s
🏠 Alternative: assisted pistol to a chair.
🍑 Hamstrings

Glute Bridge (Hamstring Bias) Beginner

Muscles affected: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back
  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, heels close to glutes.
  2. Drive through heels to lift hips, squeezing glutes.
  3. Hold 2s, lower slowly without touching down between reps.
3 sets × 15 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: none needed.

Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift Intermediate

Muscles affected: Hamstrings, glutes, lower back, balance stabilizers
  1. Stand on one leg, slight knee bend, hold a backpack.
  2. Hinge at the hips, letting the other leg extend behind you.
  3. Lower until hamstring stretch, then stand tall.
3 sets × 10/leg · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: two-leg version with both feet down.

Nordic Curl (Assisted) Advanced

Muscles affected: Hamstrings (eccentric overload), glutes, calves
  1. Kneel with ankles anchored under a couch/bed.
  2. Keep body straight from knees to head.
  3. Lower forward as slowly as possible; catch yourself with hands and push back up.
3–4 sets × 4–6 reps · Rest 120s
🏠 Alternative: slider hamstring curls with a towel on smooth floor.
🔥 Glutes

Glute Bridge Beginner

Muscles affected: Glutes, hamstrings, lower back, core
  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat, hip-width.
  2. Squeeze glutes and lift hips into a straight line knee-to-shoulder.
  3. Hold 2s at the top, lower with control.
3 sets × 15 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: donkey kicks on all fours.

Bulgarian Split Squat Intermediate

Muscles affected: Glutes, quads, hamstrings, core
  1. Rear foot on a chair, front leg forward in a lunge stance.
  2. Lower straight down until the front thigh is parallel.
  3. Drive up through the front heel.
3 sets × 8–10/leg · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: reverse lunges.

Single-Leg Hip Thrust Advanced

Muscles affected: Glutes (peak contraction), hamstrings, core
  1. Upper back on a couch edge, one foot planted, other knee to chest.
  2. Drive hips up until your torso is parallel to the floor.
  3. Squeeze glutes hard 2s at the top, lower slowly.
4 sets × 10–12/leg · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: backpack-loaded two-leg hip thrust.
🦶 Calves

Standing Calf Raise Beginner

Muscles affected: Gastrocnemius, soleus
  1. Stand tall, rise up onto the balls of your feet.
  2. Squeeze the calves hard at the top for 2s.
  3. Lower slowly through full range.
3 sets × 20 reps · Rest 45s
🏠 Alternative: single-leg version for more challenge.

Single-Leg Calf Raise Intermediate

Muscles affected: Calves (unilateral), ankle stabilizers
  1. Stand on one foot near a wall for balance only.
  2. Rise to your toes with a 2s squeeze at the top.
  3. Lower for 3s. Full range on a step edge for extra stretch.
3 sets × 15/leg · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: two-leg raises on a step.

Explosive Jump-Calf Raises Advanced

Muscles affected: Calves (fast-twitch), Achilles tendon, quads
  1. From standing, explode upward onto toes, leaving the floor slightly.
  2. Land softly and immediately repeat.
  3. Keep knees mostly straight — calves do the work.
4 sets × 20 reps · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: single-leg slow raises on a step.
🧱 Core / Abs

Plank Beginner

Muscles affected: Transverse abdominis, rectus abdominis, obliques, shoulders
  1. Forearms on the floor, elbows under shoulders, body in a straight line.
  2. Squeeze abs and glutes, don't sag hips or pike up.
  3. Breathe steadily and hold.
3 sets × 20–40s · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: knee plank or incline plank on a chair.

Crunches Beginner

Muscles affected: Rectus abdominis ("six-pack"), obliques
  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, hands beside temples.
  2. Curl shoulder blades off the floor using your abs.
  3. Lower slowly. Don't pull on your neck.
3 sets × 15 reps · Rest 45s
🏠 Alternative: dead bug on the floor.

Russian Twist Intermediate

Muscles affected: Obliques, rectus abdominis, hip flexors
  1. Sit with knees bent, lean back to ~45°, feet lifted for difficulty.
  2. Rotate your torso side to side, touching the floor.
  3. Keep chest up, core braced.
3 sets × 20 twists · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: slow Russian twist holding a water bottle.

Hollow Body Hold Advanced

Muscles affected: Entire anterior core, hip flexors
  1. Lie on your back, press lower back into the floor.
  2. Lift shoulders and legs simultaneously, arms by ears.
  3. Hold a shallow "banana" shape — no lower-back arching.
4 sets × 20–40s · Rest 60s
🏠 Alternative: hollow body with bent knees (tuck hollow).

Hanging Leg Raise Advanced

Muscles affected: Lower abs, hip flexors, grip, lats
  1. Hang from a bar with straight arms, shoulders active.
  2. Raise straight legs to at least hip height (toes to bar to advance).
  3. Lower slowly with zero swinging.
4 sets × 6–10 reps · Rest 90s
🏠 Alternative: lying leg raises on the floor.

🚀 Progression Plan — Beginner to Advanced, Then Repeat Forever

Weeks 1–4 take you from beginner to advanced. From Week 5 onward, simply repeat the advanced weekly schedule for as long as you train — it never ends. Add reps, slower tempos, or harder variations over time to keep progressing.

Week 5, 6, 7, 8… — Keep Repeating ♾️

From Week 5 onward, repeat the exact same advanced weekly schedule every week, forever. There is no end date — this is your permanent training routine.

How to keep progressing while repeating:

  • Add 1–2 reps per set each cycle, or add a set once reps plateau.
  • Slow the tempo (3–4 second lowering) or add a pause at the hardest point.
  • Load a backpack with books/water bottles to increase resistance.
  • Shorten rest by 10s when a workout feels too easy.
